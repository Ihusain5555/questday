Layer 2 — Town reveal styles

Charting a region "builds" a hand-drawn town on your map. Unexplored regions show a faint ghost of the town that inks in to full colour when you chart it — a gain, never a ruin. Two things to pick: (1) which ghost style you like, and (2) the Greymoor reframe (its mockup is a half-ruin, which reads as damage).
