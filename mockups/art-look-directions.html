Look Directions — pick one

Same cozy cottage, four art directions, all in your emerald-gold-parchment palette. This is the one decision the research couldn't make for you — it's a taste call. Each card shows how it'd be sourced and an honest "how premium does it read."

⚠️ The key truth from the research: there is no free 2D art that reads "premium painterly" as-is — the polished free sets got their polish from a 3D-prerender. So premium comes from the Grade or a 3D-prerender / commission, never from dropping a free pack in. These four are illustrative of each direction's target look (hand-drawn stand-ins), not final assets.

Pixel-art cozy

Fastest
Delivered byRoute 1 — recolor pixel CC0 (as-is)
SourceScreaming Brain Iso Town (443 tiles) · Kenney Tiny Town CC0
How it's madeEverything is built from 8px <rect> blocks snapped to a coarse grid with shape-rendering="crispEdges", so every edge is hard and stair-stepped — no anti-aliasing, no gradients. The isometric read comes from offsetting each row of blocks by 8px to fake 2:1 diamond faces (top, lit-left, shaded-right). Each face uses a strict 2-3 tone flat ramp from one hue family: cream cube (white-cream highlight / parchment / khaki-shade), gold roof (pale ridge / bright-left / dark-right) with 2px shingle step-lines, and emerald grass/tree (deep / mid / bright). Tiny touches — a glowing gold window, a green plank door with a gold handle, an emerald-brick chimney with stacked cream smoke puffs, and a blocky tree — sell the cozy 16-bit town-tile feel.
Premium readReads cozy & retro "sim-game," NOT premium-fantasy. Fastest to ship; clashes with your clean Clash-Display/Satoshi brand.

Clean flat-vector

Best brand-fit
Delivered byVector-iso — recolor + extend one CC0 SVG kit
SourceArtyom Zagorskiy "Isometric Medieval Pack" (269 SVG tiles, CC0) CC0 (true vector)
How it's madeBuilt on a true 2:1 isometric grid (every diamond is twice as wide as tall): the ground patch, cube footprint, and roof ridge all follow the same 26.6 degree slope. Each face is a single flat solid color with no gradients — the emerald palette is mapped to light/dark by face orientation only (bright #2fb380 catches light, #1f8a60 mid, deep #10362a in shadow), which is the one subtle "shade" the style allows. Every shape carries a thin consistent gold (#f5b938 / soft #ffcf5c) outline at uniform 2.5px with rounded joins, so it reads like a Phosphor-icon-adjacent vector. Door, window panes, lantern, tree, and grass tufts are all polygons aligned to the iso axes; near-invisible 6-10% opacity overlays add depth without breaking the flat look.
Premium readClean & modern-premium; unifies best with your Phosphor/SVG brand. Coverage is thin (one kit) — needs in-house extension for an Empire-scale world.

Painterly 2.5D (3D-prerender look)

★ Recommended primary
Delivered byRoute 2 — pre-render CC0 3D kits (Blender) ⭐
SourceKayKit Medieval Builder (200+) · Kenney Town/Castle/Base 3D — CC0 CC0
How it's madeEvery face carries its own multi-stop linear/radial gradient running light-to-dark (cream walls sunlit on the left, ochre walls in shade on the right; emerald roof bright on the lit slope, near-black emerald on the shaded slope). A consistent top-left key light drives a soft warm rim-light: pale-mint and cream strokes trace the ridge crest, wall-top edges, door and window frames. Ambient occlusion is painted as low-opacity dark polygons in the crevices — where the eaves overhang the walls, along the inner vertical corner, and at wall bases. A blurred dark ellipse under the house plus a faint contact-line gives the soft grounded cast shadow, and a turbulence-noise filter lays subtle speckle texture on the plaster walls. The emerald-glazed roof, gold-lit windows, hanging lantern, and a rim-lit tree complete the cozy gains-only mood.
Premium readThe highest premium ceiling reachable with free art — "polished mobile city-builder." Controllable emerald-gold lighting. One-time Blender setup (AI-scripted, you judge the look).

Storybook parchment

Best tone / Phase 2 moat
Delivered byGrade direction + commission hero pieces (Phase 2)
SourceCC0 base + your Grade; AI-to-style or a freelance buyout for hero art CC0 + owned
How it's madeCream/parchment radial backdrop with a subtle fractal-noise paper grain overlay sells the hand-illustrated page. Everything is drawn in true 2:1 isometric (dx:dy = 2:1) — the ground diamond, the cube-shaped house body, and the gable roof all follow the same axis so the silhouette reads as a real iso building. Warmth comes from honey-gold roof and a softly glowing doorway/window; cozy rounded forms (pillowy tree clusters, rounded chimney smoke curl, grass tufts, drifting birds) keep it picture-book. The signature storybook touch is the chunky dark-emerald ink outline (#10362a) on every shape with rounded joins/caps, mimicking a hand-drawn pen line, plus soft warm drop-shadows instead of hard ones — never grim, always inviting.
Premium readWarmest, most "cozy gains-only" tone and the most ownable/defensible — but true painterly needs a commission or AI-to-style step (not free 2D).

My recommendation

  • Primary: the Painterly 2.5D direction via Route 2 (pre-render CC0 3D kits). It's the only path to a genuinely premium look that stays free/CC0 and zero-dependency — and the firm-up confirmed it's realistic for me to set up while you just judge the renders.
  • If you want the cleanest brand match instead → the Flat-Vector direction (Artyom Zagorskiy's CC0 SVG kit), accepting thinner coverage we'd extend over time.
  • Phase 2 moat: commission a few hero pieces in the Storybook tone once the design is proven — owned, defensible art on top of the CC0 base.
  • Pixel is the fastest but reads "retro sim," not premium — best only as a quick prototype.

Accessibility note baked into the plan: emerald+gold is the hardest pairing for red-green colorblindness, so the real build never uses color alone — every town/state/region also gets a label, icon, or shape, with a colorblind-safe blue reserved for the "current" highlight.
