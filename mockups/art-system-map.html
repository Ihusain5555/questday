QuestDay Art System — how it works

The big idea: your art isn't one job, it's a 9-stage pipeline. You lean on free CC0 art for almost everything and build only one thin custom layer — the Grade — that makes it all look like one premium world.

1 · SourceGrab free CC0 art (Kenney & friends) — buildings, map, textures, audio, particles.
➞
2 · Grade ★One treatment stamps everything: recolor, light, shadow → emerald-gold-parchment.
➞
3 · RenderPlain SVG map + Canvas town draw the finished, cohesive world. No engine.
The spine: anchor on ONE CC0 ecosystem — Kenney.nl alone covers art, UI, particles & audio under one identical CC0 license, so "make mismatched free stuff match" is half-solved before you start.

Licensing — what's safe to ship in your installer

✅ CC0 — bundle freely, no credit (Kenney, Screaming Brain, ambientCG, Poly Haven, Freesound-CC0) ◐ CC-BY — ship-safe WITH a one-line credit (game-icons.net, Twemoji) ⛔ AVOID — CraftPix, Unity/Unreal store packs, Inkarnate, Wonderdraft assets, Habitica art, raw AI as your signature
  • One acceptance rule going forward: bundle a pack only if its page explicitly says CC0 (or CC-BY with a credit), and save the license text next to the files.
  • Zero new dependencies: every "Build" stage runs offline at build time or uses browser-native features — nothing new ships in the installer.
  • The only open question is the LOOK (see the look-directions mockup): does recolored CC0 read premium, or do we pre-render free 3D kits / commission a few hero pieces?
